# Hi, I'm Aditya 👋

I build and evaluate agentic AI systems. As a data scientist and AI solutions lead at Capgemini, I work with enterprise client teams to take AI ideas from discovery to production, with a focus on two things: grounding models in the right knowledge, and proving they work before anyone relies on them.

## What I work on

- **Knowledge-grounded retrieval.** Built GraphRAG search over 350K+ lease documents, modeling agreements and their amendments as a Neo4j graph. It reached ~96% accuracy on a client-provided golden set, versus ~36% for vector search.
- **Agent tooling.** Built a Bitbucket MCP server that lets AI agents search and review code, commit changes and raise PRs, and a graph-based knowledge hub that gives our SDLC agent the context it needs to help automate builds and deployment.
- **LLM evaluation.** Golden sets, LLM-as-judge scoring, reference-free Ragas metrics and human review, chosen to fit the problem, including ones with no single right answer.
- **Teaching.** I run AI office hours on model choice, building skills, working within context limits and agentic coding setups, often through live coding.

Before Capgemini, I built churn models and ran A/B tests on 200M+ marketplace interactions at Upwork (8% relative lift in client retention), and worked on product and analytics at PassML, AlphaMoney and Deloitte.
